Decide whether the laboratory may and can take the work, on terms both sides understand. Nothing technical starts until this stage closes.

Controlled documents PR-CTR-01 CHK-CTR-01 FR-COI-02
1.1 Register and screen the request Complete

Log the request before any technical discussion, so the review has a start date and a reference. Anything outside SC-LAB-01 is excluded or subcontracted — it cannot be absorbed.

  • Request logged with date received, client, contact and the client's own reference; request document saved to 00_Contract
  • Scope checked against SC-LAB-01: out-of-scope items excluded, or subcontracting agreed with the client's written approval (PR-EXT-01)
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: 08 Oct 2026
1.2 Impartiality, confidentiality and capability Complete

Ask specifically whether the laboratory or any assigned person designed, consulted on, sold or previously certified this DUT or its firmware. Check calibration against the planned job end, not today.

  • FR-COI-02 completed with prior involvement in this DUT, its firmware or the vendor explicitly checked; any risk recorded in RG-RSK-01 with its mitigation, or the work declined
  • FR-CONF-02 and FR-CONF-01 on file; authorized person in RG-CMP-01, equipment in RG-EQP-01 valid past the planned job end, and the required method all confirmed available
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: 09 Oct 2026
1.3 Contract, decision rule and authorization to test Complete

Ambiguity written here is the single largest cause of dispute later. For security testing the authorization letter is what makes the testing lawful and bounded.

  • CHK-CTR-01 completed in full; DUT, versions, services, interfaces in and out of scope, environment, dates and deliverables documented; decision rule and statement-of-conformity basis agreed in writing (ISO 7.1.3)
  • Written Authorization to Test on file stating rules of engagement, interfaces and IP ranges, test window, whether destructive testing is permitted, data handling and escalation contact — and CR-2026-XXX logged with Decision = Accept
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: 12 Oct 2026

Controlled documents IXIT doc §1–2 Mapping G–I
3.1 Identify the DUT Complete

§2 of the IXIT document is the unambiguous identification ISO 7.8.2 will require in the report. The constrained-device answer alone changes the applicability of several provisions.

  • IXIT document §1 document control complete (standard, test specification, scope, methodology, project ID, prepared and reviewed by, classification, version, date); file named for the job in Test Report › [03] Forms
  • §2 complete: manufacturer, model, hardware revision, serial, MAC, firmware as received and at end of assessment, interfaces, management address, default credential source, companion app, supplied material — anything not supplied marked “Not provided” with the reason, never left blank
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: 17 Oct 2026

  • ICS Support (G) returned Y / N / N-A for every method whose Conditions column states a condition, attributable to who declared it and when
  • Applicable? (H) set for all 63 methods with its basis in (I); every N and N-A challenged against its condition and against §2; clarifications logged in tracker 06C and closed
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: 19 Oct 2026
Gate 3Passed

Collect the implementation detail the methods need, and confirm it is good enough to assess against. One entry per real thing — a single-entry IXIT silently collapses the assessment into one untraceable test.

Controlled documents IXIT doc IXIT 1–29 Mapping R
4.1 Collect the IXIT Complete

Reject records that say “all interfaces are secured”. Every mechanism, component, interface, parameter and service gets its own row and its own ID. IXIT 1–29 are filled in the job copy of IXIT_and_DUT_Identification_Template.docx, in Test Report › [03] Forms.

  • Required IXIT tables identified from Required IXIT Entries (R) across every method with Applicable = Y
  • IXIT 1–29 completed in the job copy: one entry per mechanism keeping its template ID, information source recorded per entry, unused template rows deleted, and dependencies cross-referenced by ID (AuthMech → SecParam, ComMech → NetSecImpl, SoftServ → Intf, UpdMech → UpdProc)
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: 23 Oct 2026
4.2 Adequacy review and approval Complete

Adequacy observations are part of the assessment record and may be reported. Missing information is an information gap — it is never an N/A.

  • Every entry checked against the fields column R asks for; entries that are vague, inconsistent or contradict §2 recorded in tracker 06C with their effect on the verdict
  • Consolidated clarification issued with owner and due date, responses re-reviewed and the gap rows closed; no required entry left empty; IXIT document approved at an issue version and recorded in tracker 04A
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: 27 Oct 2026
Gate 4Passed
Every IXIT entry named by an applicable method is present at entry-per-mechanism granularity, with its information source recorded, and the IXIT document is approved at an issue version.

Controlled documents PR-MET-01 Mapping Tracker 06
5.1 Confirm the methods and settle deviations Complete

Methods are controlled documents — never edit a method's steps or acceptance criteria inside a job copy. A deviation authorized after the fact is a nonconformity, not a deviation.

  • Job copy taken from the current controlled mapping revision, with columns A–F, J, K, P and R untouched
  • Verification evidence on first use for standard methods; modified or laboratory-developed methods validated and authorized under PR-MET-01, with the record in 05_Review_QC before use
  • Every applicable method has the IXIT entries column R names present and adequate; any departure from a method authorized in advance in tracker 06A with its justification and its effect on the verdict
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: 29 Oct 2026

  • Plan approved by the Technical Reviewer and recorded with name and date in 01 Job Record
Responsible: M. AlshalanDate: open since 29 Oct

Controlled documents Mapping L · O Tracker 05 Tracker 06
6.1 Baseline the DUT and assess conceptually Not started

Every later observation is relative to the baseline, so capture it before you start. Write the reasoning, not just the conclusion.

  • DUT baseline set and captured as evidence: factory reset or documented configuration, firmware version, network topology
  • Conceptual assessment of the IXIT entries in column R against the acceptance criteria in column K, with the reasoning written in Tester Notes (S); insufficient information recorded as a gap in tracker 06C
6.2 Test the DUT and register the evidence Not started

An unrecorded deviation invalidates the result. Register each file once — never twice under two Evidence IDs, and never cite an unregistered artefact.

  • Functional assessment executed in the controlled setup using only RG-EQP-01 equipment, with the IDs actually used recorded in tracker 03; any unplanned deviation authorized in 06A, or work stopped and NC-2026-XXX raised, before continuing
  • Each evidence file registered once in tracker 05 with ID, type, description, location, SHA-256, date, collector, related method and IXIT IDs, confidentiality and retention; stored in 03_Evidence under its Evidence ID
6.3 Set the verdicts and run quality control Not started

The verdict comes from the acceptance criteria, not from impression. N-A traces to a declaration, never to missing information.

  • Verdict (Tester) (L) set for every method with Applicable = Y, taken strictly from column K, with Evidence ID(s) (O) populated; N-A used only where it traces to the ICS, INCONCLUSIVE where information is missing and testing cannot resolve it
  • Planned QC performed under PR-QC-01 and logged in tracker 06B and Process_Control > QC_Activities; verdict counts entered per provision group in tracker 04B
Gate 6Not reached
Every applicable method has a tester verdict, every verdict names at least one registered Evidence ID, every evidence file is registered once with a hash, and planned QC is logged.

Confirm independently that the records support the verdicts, before anything is reported. The reviewer did not perform the work.

Controlled documents Mapping M · N Tracker 04 Tracker 05
7.1 Confirm independence and completeness Not started

  • Every method with Applicable = Y carries a tester verdict; every Applicable = N has a basis in (I) that matches the ICS declaration in (G)
7.2 Check traceability and correctness, then sign Not started

Traceability is what an assessor will test hardest: can any single verdict be followed back to a file with a hash? The Final Verdict column is what the report reproduces.

  • Every tested method names an Evidence ID that exists in tracker 05; every file in 03_Evidence is registered with a matching SHA-256; every IXIT entry a verdict rests on is in the approved IXIT document
  • No PASS the recorded evidence does not support; every deviation authorized in advance; every INCONCLUSIVE states what was missing and what would resolve it; equipment calibration valid on the test date; planned QC performed
  • Verdict (Reviewer) (M) and Final Verdict (N) set for every tested method, anything not accepted returned and re-reviewed; tracker 04 reconciliation reads OK; review record signed and stored in 05_Review_QC
Gate 7Not reached

Report the results accurately, clearly and unambiguously, with a conformity statement that rests on the agreed decision rule.

Controlled documents PR-REP-01 Report template
8.1 Draft the report Not started

ISO 7.8.2 content is not negotiable, and a reader must be able to see what was not tested and why.

  • ISO 7.8.2 content present: laboratory identity and where the work was performed, client, unambiguous item identification with hardware and firmware versions, dates of receipt and of testing, methods and any deviations or exclusions, results, the person authorizing the report, and the statement that results relate only to the items tested
  • Results reported per provision with verdict, method applied and basis; Not Applicable provisions shown with their ICS justification; INCONCLUSIVE verdicts and material IXIT observations carried forward
  • Decision rule applied and stated with the risk it accepts; the scope of the conformity statement stated; opinions and interpretations marked as such and issued only by authorized personnel; any subcontracted result and its provider identified
8.2 Finalize, approve and issue Not started

  • Every {{...}} placeholder replaced, Annex T deleted, §4 counts and figures taken from tracker 04, and TLP and classification consistent on the cover, in the header and in the footer
  • Checked against the reviewed records and signed by the Authorized Signatory; REP-2026-XXX logged in Process_Control > Reports_Index; issued through the agreed channel with the transmittal recorded in 04_Report
