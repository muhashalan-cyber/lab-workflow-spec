- Item | Content | Reference
- Document set for one job
- IXIT & DUT Identification Template (.docx) | DUT identification + IXIT 1–29 | ETSI steps 1 and 3
- This file — Method / Test Mapping (.xlsx) | ICS declaration, applicability, method detail, verdicts | ETSI steps 2, 4, 5, 6, 8

- Columns you fill in (highlighted yellow)
- G  ICS Support | Supplier declaration against the condition in column F: Y / N / N-A | ETSI step 2 (ICS). N/A verdicts must trace to a Y/N here — never to missing information.
- H  Applicable? | Lab decision from ICS + IXIT: Y / N | ETSI step 4. Blank is not an answer; every method gets Y or N.
- I  Applicability Basis | Why the method is applicable or not — cite the ICS declaration or the IXIT entry | Challenge and record every N.
- L  Verdict (Tester) | PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE / N-A / NOT TESTED | ETSI step 8. INCONCLUSIVE where information is missing and testing cannot resolve it.
- M  Verdict (Reviewer) | Independent technical review of the tester verdict | ISO 7.8.7 — reviewer must not be the tester.
- N  Final Verdict | The verdict reported, after review | Feeds §4 and §5.2 of the report and sheet 04 of the tracker.
- O  Evidence ID(s) | EV-XXX from the tracker Evidence Register | ISO 7.5.1 — reference registered evidence, never an unregistered file.
- Q  Decision Rule Applies? | Y / N — per the rule agreed at contract review | ISO 7.8.6
- S  Tester Notes | Working notes | Not a substitute for registered evidence.
- Columns that are controlled reference content — do not edit per job
- A–F, J, K, P, R | Method ID, provision, test, TS ref, M/R, conditions, steps, acceptance criteria, tools, required IXIT entries | Change these only by revising this template under document control (ISO 8.3).
- M / R codes (column E)
- Code | Meaning
- M | the provision is a mandatory requirement
- R | the provision is a recommendation
- M C | mandatory requirement and conditional — applicability depends on column F
- R C | recommendation and conditional — applicability depends on column F
